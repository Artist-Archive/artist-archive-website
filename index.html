Step two · the weeks in between

Nothing left to remember

A season of weddings starts to blur. Open the client and it is all still there: the foundation, the shade, how much lash, where the parting sat.

Questions

The things people ask

Is it only for bridal?

It grew from bridal because that is what I was doing when I came up with this solution. However, anything with a trial, appointments, a date and a face works the same way, and a lot of it is simply a good record of what you used on somebody.

Does it do bookings and payments?

No, and that is on purpose. There are already apps for taking bookings and money. This is the part nobody built: what you did, what you used, and what they asked for.

Is it for hairstylists too?

Yes. The consultation records hair as well as makeup, and you tell it which you do or if you do both.

What happens to my photos?

They stay yours. Nobody else can see your clients or their photos, and you can download or delete them whenever you like. The privacy policy explains exactly what is stored and where.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything saves to your phone first and syncs when you are back on a connection.
