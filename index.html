The problem

The trial was two months ago

What lipstick did you use? The exact foundation shade? Where the parting sat, which extensions, how long did the curl last? Trial details scattered in your head, on paper, in your notes app, buried in the camera roll, lost in old emails.

Artist Archive gives all of it one home.

What it does

Every detail, in one place

Every client

A home for every client

Keep a profile for each bride, with consultation details, allergies and a countdown to the big day. Every trial you log sits with them, so their whole history is one tap away.

Log every trial

Build your kit once, then just tick

No more typing every product by hand. Your kit shows one row per product, and you tap what you used. A built-in catalog of 7,300 products, and growing, means you are not typing shades by hand either.

The whole look

Every trial, ready to recreate

Each trial holds the products and shades you used, how the hair was styled, your notes, the little adjustments, and photos of the finished look. Weeks later it is all there, nothing left to memory.

Find it again

Any past look, in seconds

Every photo you take sits in one browsable gallery. Tap any look to jump straight back to the trial that made it, the products, the shades and the notes all still there.
