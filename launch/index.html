Questions

The things people ask

Is it only for bridal?

It grew out of bridal because that is the work it came from. But anything with a trial, a date and a face works the same way, and a lot of it is simply a good record of what you used on somebody.

Does it do bookings and payments?

No, and that is on purpose. There are good apps for taking bookings and money. This is the part nobody built: what you did, what you used, and what they asked for.

Is it for hairstylists too?

Yes. The consultation records hair as well as makeup, and you tell it which you do. Or both.

What happens to my photos?

They stay yours. Nobody else can see your clients or their photos, and you can download or delete them whenever you like. The privacy policy spells out exactly what is stored and where.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything saves to your phone first and syncs when you are back on a connection, which matters in a stone villa with one bar of signal.
