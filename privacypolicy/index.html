Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 July 2026
Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. About this privacy policy

Artist Archive is a mobile app designed to help makeup artists, hairstylists and hair and makeup artists keep organised records of their clients, trials, appointments, products, notes and photographs.

In this privacy policy:

  • “Artist Archive”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Stephanie Garrett, trading as Artist Archive.
  • “You” and “your” refer to the artist who creates and uses an Artist Archive account.
  • “Client” refers to a person whose information is entered into the app by an artist.

This privacy policy explains what personal information Artist Archive collects, why it is collected, how it is used and protected, who it may be shared with, and the rights individuals have in relation to it.

Artist Archive is operated by Stephanie Garrett as a sole trader based in New Zealand.

Artist Archive is subject to the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Other privacy laws, including the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Australian Privacy Act 1988, may also apply in some circumstances.

2. Information about artists and clients

Artist Archive handles two main types of personal information.

Information about you as an artist

This includes the information needed to create, manage and secure your Artist Archive account.

For this information, Artist Archive is responsible for deciding how and why the information is handled.

Information about your clients

This includes client details, consultation notes, appointment records, products and photographs that you choose to enter into the app.

You decide what client information is collected and entered into Artist Archive. You are responsible for ensuring that you have a lawful and appropriate reason to collect, store and use that information.

Artist Archive stores and processes client information on your behalf so that we can provide the app.

Where the GDPR applies, you will generally be the data controller of your clients’ information and Artist Archive will act as your data processor.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information

When you create and use an Artist Archive account, we may collect:

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, if you choose to provide it.
  • Your professional role, such as makeup artist, hairstylist or both.
  • Your account creation date.
  • Your email verification status.
  • Basic account and subscription information.
  • Information needed to authenticate and secure your account.

If you create an account using an email address and password, your password is managed by our authentication provider. Artist Archive does not receive or store your password in readable form.

If sign-in through Apple or Google is available and you use that option, we may receive confirmation of your identity and limited account information, such as your name or email address, depending on the information you have chosen to share through that provider.

We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

3.2 Client information entered by artists

The information you choose to enter about a client may include:

  • The client’s name.
  • Their phone number or email address.
  • Event or appointment dates.
  • Preparation locations and other event details.
  • Consultation notes.
  • Skin type and undertone.
  • Hair texture, thickness and length.
  • Allergy or sensitivity information.
  • Details of trials, sessions and appointments.
  • Products used.
  • Adjustments or changes made to a look.
  • Tags and additional notes.
  • Photographs.
  • A record of whether the client has consented to you publicly sharing their photographs.

You control what information you enter. You should only record information that is reasonably necessary for your work and that you are permitted to collect and store.

3.3 Photographs

When you add a photograph to Artist Archive, the image is uploaded to and stored using our cloud storage provider.

The app may compress or resize photographs before uploading them. We may also store information associated with a photograph, including:

  • The date it was added.
  • Its file type or file size.
  • The client or session it relates to.
  • A label or description you have added.

Photographs of identifiable people are personal information.

Artist Archive does not currently use facial recognition or use client photographs to identify, authenticate or profile people.

3.4 Device permissions

Depending on the features you use, Artist Archive may request permission to access:

  • Your camera, so you can take a photo of a look or the products used directly in the app.
  • Your photo library, so you can select and upload existing photographs.
  • Notifications, if app notifications are offered.

These permissions are used only to provide the feature you choose to use.

You can manage device permissions through your phone or device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Artist Archive does not automatically access or upload your entire photo library. Artist Archive does not access your microphone.

3.5 Technical and service information

We and our technology providers may automatically process limited technical information needed to operate, secure and maintain the app, including:

  • Your app version.
  • Device and operating-system information.
  • Login and authentication events.
  • Network and security information.
  • Dates and times of service requests.
  • Error information.
  • Records used to detect misuse, investigate faults and maintain security.

We do not use this information for behavioural advertising.

Artist Archive uses a crash-reporting service (Google Firebase Crashlytics) to record technical information when the app crashes or encounters a serious error, such as device and operating-system details, the app version, and diagnostic information about the error. This is used only to find and fix faults and to keep the app stable and secure. It is not used for advertising.

If we introduce a separate app analytics service in the future, we will update this privacy policy before or when that service is introduced.

3.6 Support communications

If you contact Artist Archive for support, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • The contents of your message.
  • Screenshots or other information you choose to provide.
  • Relevant account or technical information needed to investigate your request.

Please avoid sending client information through ordinary email unless it is genuinely necessary for us to assist you.

3.7 Website and waitlist information

If you visit the Artist Archive website, contact us through the website or join a waitlist, we may collect information such as:

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, if requested.
  • The contents of a contact form or message.
  • The date and source of your registration.
  • Basic website and device information.
  • Basic cookie information, where applicable.

We use waitlist information to provide launch news and other updates you have requested. You can unsubscribe from these communications at any time using the unsubscribe option in the email or by contacting us.

3.8 Information we do not collect or use

Artist Archive does not:

  • Sell artists’ or clients’ personal information.
  • Rent personal information to other businesses.
  • Use client records or photographs for advertising.
  • Use client photographs to train public artificial-intelligence models.
  • Use facial recognition on client photographs.
  • Allow other artists to access your client records.
  • Store your complete payment-card details.

3.9 Anonymous product statistics

Artist Archive keeps anonymous counts of how often products appear across the app, so we can see which products and shades artists use most.

For each count we record the brand, the product name, the shade, the month, and whether the account belongs to an artist working in makeup or hair. We also record what the count relates to, such as a product being added to a kit or a wishlist, being marked as needing restocking, or being recorded as used in an appointment.

These counts are not linked to you. No account identifier, name, email address, device identifier or location is stored with them. There is no way for us to trace a count back to an individual account, and we cannot use them to see any artist’s kit.

We use these counts to improve the product catalogue and to understand which products artists need. They may also inform conversations with brands about how products are used across the app as a whole. Only overall totals would be involved. We do not share your kit, your clients, your photographs or any other information from your account for this purpose.

These counts are not used for advertising, and they are not used to track you across other apps or websites.

We will not use personal information for a materially different purpose without providing appropriate notice and, where legally required, obtaining consent.

  • Operate and secure the service.
  • Prevent misuse.
  • Investigate faults.
  • Improve the service’s reliability.
  • Respond to enquiries.

We will only rely on legitimate interests where those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.

Consent

We may rely on consent for optional activities, such as certain marketing communications or non-essential website cookies.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Legal obligations

We may process information where required to comply with an applicable law or lawful regulatory requirement.

As the artist entering information about your clients, you are responsible for identifying an appropriate legal basis for your own collection, storage and use of that information.

These providers may process information only to provide their services to Artist Archive, subject to their applicable contractual, security and privacy obligations.

Artist Archive uses cloud-based technology providers.

Artist Archive’s Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage data is stored in Sydney, Australia.

Information may also be processed from other countries where our technology providers, support providers or their authorised personnel operate.

This means personal information may be stored or processed outside New Zealand.

When we use an overseas provider to store or process information solely on our behalf, Artist Archive remains responsible for taking reasonable steps to ensure that the information is appropriately protected.

Where an overseas transfer is subject to the GDPR, we will rely on an approved transfer mechanism where required, such as standard contractual clauses or another legally recognised safeguard.

8. Artists’ responsibilities for client information

Artist Archive is a professional record-keeping tool. It does not replace your own privacy notices, consent processes or professional responsibilities.

When entering client information, you are responsible for:

  • Collecting only information that you reasonably need.
  • Explaining to clients why you are collecting their information where required.
  • Ensuring you have a lawful and fair basis for collecting and storing it.
  • Obtaining any consent required by law or appropriate professional practice.
  • Keeping the information accurate where reasonably possible.
  • Protecting any information you export, download, copy or share.
  • Responding to requests from your clients.
  • Deleting information when you no longer need to retain it.
  • Complying with the privacy laws that apply to your business and clients.

Allergy and sensitivity information

Allergy and sensitivity notes may reveal information about a person’s health.

You should only record this information where it is relevant to providing your service and where you have appropriately informed the client or obtained consent when required.

Client photographs

Before storing an identifiable client photograph, you should ensure that the client understands that the photograph will be stored in Artist Archive for professional record-keeping purposes.

Consent to store a photograph as part of a private professional record is separate from consent to publish, advertise with or publicly share that photograph.

Photo-consent setting

The app may allow you to record whether a client has consented to public use of their photographs.

This setting is provided only as a record-keeping aid. Selecting or changing the setting does not itself obtain valid consent from a client.

You remain responsible for obtaining and recording appropriate permission before posting or publicly sharing a photograph.

Client privacy requests

If a client asks to access, correct or delete information that you have entered about them, you are generally responsible for responding to that request.

The app allows you to view, update and delete client records. You may contact Artist Archive if you need reasonable technical assistance to action a request.

9. How we protect information

Artist Archive uses administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect personal information.

These safeguards may include:

  • Authentication and account-access controls.
  • Database security rules designed to restrict users to their own records.
  • Encryption during transmission.
  • Encryption of information stored by our cloud provider.
  • Managed authentication services.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Security monitoring and technical logging.
  • Processes for responding to suspected privacy or security incidents.

You are also responsible for protecting your account. You should:

  • Use a strong and unique password.
  • Keep your device and email account secure.
  • Avoid sharing account credentials.
  • Sign out of shared devices.
  • Contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. However, we will take reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information we hold.

10. How long information is kept

We generally retain your account information and app records for as long as your account remains active or as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service.

You control how long most client records remain in your account.

Deleting individual records

You can delete individual clients, sessions, notes and photographs through the app.

Once deletion is processed, the information will no longer be available through your account.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in backups, security records or technical systems before being automatically removed or overwritten in accordance with our providers’ normal retention processes.

Deleting your account

You may request or initiate deletion of your account through the app where that feature is available, or by contacting us.

Account deletion removes your active Artist Archive account and the client records, sessions, products and photographs associated with it.

Some limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary to:

  • Complete the deletion process.
  • Maintain security and fraud-prevention records.
  • Resolve a dispute or support request.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Maintain temporary system backups.

Any information retained for these purposes will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary and will not be used for unrelated purposes.

Before deleting your account, you should make any copies you are legally permitted to keep and wish to retain. Account deletion may not be reversible.

The rights available to you depend on the law that applies.

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you may ask Artist Archive to:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information about you.
  • Provide access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct personal information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

You can update or delete much of your information directly through the app.

Where another applicable law provides additional rights, you may also have the right to:

  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Restrict certain processing.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain to an appropriate privacy regulator.

Privacy rights may be subject to legal limitations or exceptions.

To make a request, contact the Artist Archive Privacy Officer using the details in section 18.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Requests concerning information entered by another artist should generally be directed to that artist first, because the artist controls the client record.

12. Children and young people

Artist Archive is intended for professional makeup artists and hairstylists and is not designed for children to create accounts.

We do not knowingly allow children to create Artist Archive accounts.

If we become aware that a child has created an account without appropriate authorisation, we may suspend or delete the account and associated information.

Artists may work with clients who are children or young people. If you enter information about a client who is a minor, you are responsible for:

  • Ensuring the collection is appropriate.
  • Providing information to the young person in an age-appropriate way where applicable.
  • Obtaining consent from the young person, their parent or guardian where appropriate or required.
  • Using particular care when storing or publicly sharing their photographs.

13. Privacy and security breaches

Artist Archive maintains processes for responding to suspected privacy and security breaches.

If a breach occurs, we will investigate it and take reasonable steps to contain and address it.

Where a privacy breach has caused, or is likely to cause, serious harm, we will notify the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals where required by the Privacy Act 2020.

We will also comply with applicable notification requirements under other privacy laws where those requirements apply.
